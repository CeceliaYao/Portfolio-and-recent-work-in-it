Hello, I'm Cecelia

Designer who writes the code.

I'm Qinxi, though most people call me Cecelia. I study Interactive and Visual Design at QUT in Brisbane.

I want to work as an interaction designer and creative technologist who turns personal stories and everyday experiences into clear, playful digital interactions. I value curiosity, empathy and experimentation.

My goal is to combine visual thinking with code, build experiences people can understand intuitively, and create work that feels both useful and emotionally memorable.
