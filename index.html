    About

    Designer who writes the code.

    I'm Qinxi, though most people call me Cecelia. I study Interactive and Visual Design at QUT in Brisbane.

    I design a bit like a director, with colour, light and rhythm, and then I build the thing so it moves. Lately that means pixel games, speculative interfaces and rooms full of memories.

    Away from the desk I'm on a trail, mixing a drink or rearranging this room.
