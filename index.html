    About

    Designer who writes the code.

    I'm Qinxi, though most people call me Cecelia. I study Interactive and Visual Design at QUT in Brisbane.

    My design philosophy is rooted in visual storytelling and emotional resonance. I build an atmosphere much like a director, using colour, light and rhythm, and then I write the code that makes it move.

    Away from the desk I go hiking, taste whisky and gin, collect a little Chinese culture and a little Picasso, and keep something green by the window. They all end up in the work.
