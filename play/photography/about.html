About Qinxi Yao

Interaction Designer & Visual Storyteller

Introduction

My name is Qinxi Yao!

An undergraduate in interaction and visual design with a passion for creating meaningful digital experiences through the lens of both technology and artistry.

Education & Background

Bachelor of Creative Industries (Interactive Design)

QUT, Brisbane, Australia

My studies focus on interaction design, combining user experience principles with creative problem-solving to craft meaningful digital experiences.

I was born and raised in southern China. When I was a child, I was always attracted to visual arts and photography.

Since beginning my studies at QUT, I've developed a passion for interaction design, learning to think creatively about engaging digital interactions. I've come to realise that we must consider the user needs, aesthetics and technical feasibility to provide.

As I am halfway through my learning journey, I'm excited to explore the possibilities and can't wait to start a career in the design industry!
